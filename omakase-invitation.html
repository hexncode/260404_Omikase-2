おまかせ
Omakase · Nine Courses
料理長 · Chef Henry
一 · First Course
白味噌仕立て
Shiro Miso Soup
A first sip to settle the table — white miso broth drawn from kombu and bonito, with silken tofu, wakame, and fine spring onion.
二 · Second Course
生牡蠣
Sydney Rock Oysters, Two Ways
Four oysters on shaved ice — two pristine with yuzu kosho and lime, two dressed with cucumber-ginger mignonette and a pearl of ikura.
三 · Third Course
鮭といくらのパイ包み
Salmon & Roe Pastry Parcel
A golden, hand-folded pastry baked to a flaky shatter — filled with cured salmon and salmon roe, finished with the chef's secret sauce.
四 · Fourth Course
刺身盛り合わせ
Sashimi Trio · Salmon & Snapper
Tasmanian salmon usuzukuri, torched salmon belly with sea salt and lemon, and snapper hira-zukuri over grated daikon with ponzu.
五 · Fifth Course
手巻き三種
Hand Rolls · A Trio
Three crisp nori cones rolled to order — spicy salmon & cucumber, snapper-avocado with chive oil, and "negitoro-style" salmon belly with pickled daikon.
六 · Sixth Course
鯛の昆布締め
Snapper Kombujime
Snapper pressed between sheets of kombu until it firms and turns silky, sliced thin with yuzu, finishing salt, and a single shiso leaf.
七 · Seventh Course
西京焼き鮭
Saikyo Miso Glazed Salmon
Salmon fillet marinated two days in white miso and sake, grilled until lacquered and just-flaking, with charred lemon and pickled ginger.
八 · Eighth Course
握り四貫
Nigiri · Four Pieces
Hand-pressed shari topped four ways — classic salmon, aburi salmon with yuzu kosho, snapper with yuzu salt, and salmon-ikura gunkan.
九 · Ninth Course
ほうじ茶プリン
Hojicha Pudding, Kuromitsu
A trembling roasted-tea custard pooled with dark sugar syrup, crowned with toasted soybean flour and a salted cherry blossom.
